## 1. Assets

Subject to the exclusions in this Agreement, the Seller agrees to transfer the project assets delivered for BidPilot, including source code, application files, project-specific documentation, project-specific UI assets, demo data, API contracts, data-model documentation, production checklist, acquisition documentation, and the repository handoff identified by the parties.

## 2. Purchase price

Purchase price: **USD 5,000**, one-time.

Unless otherwise agreed in writing, the suggested schedule is 40% at signing, 30% after source-code handoff and functional review, 20% after deployment/handoff validation, and 10% at closing.

## 3. Intellectual property

At closing and subject to receipt of the agreed purchase price, Seller assigns to Acquirer the transferable intellectual-property rights owned by Seller in the project-specific assets included in the sale, to the extent permitted by law.

## 4. Repository and delivery

The parties will identify the final repository, branch and delivery method before closing. Seller will provide reasonable access needed to complete the agreed handoff.

## 5. Derivative works and commercial rights

Subject to applicable law and third-party exclusions, Acquirer may modify, commercialize, distribute, sublicense, rebrand and create derivative works from the transferred project-specific assets.

## 6. Third-party and open-source materials

Third-party and open-source software is excluded from any ownership transfer except for the license rights granted by its applicable license. Acquirer must comply with those licenses.

## 7. Prototype status

Acquirer acknowledges that the product is a working prototype and that AI, authentication, database, document parsing, storage, notifications, billing, security controls and procurement integrations may require additional implementation.

## 8. No ongoing services

No ongoing development, support, hosting or maintenance is included unless separately agreed in writing.

## 9. Confidentiality

The parties will protect confidential information exchanged in connection with the transaction as required by applicable law and any separate confidentiality agreement.
